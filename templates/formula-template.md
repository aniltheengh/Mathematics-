Authoring template: use for formulas that need independent reference pages.
Simple formulas can remain in their canonical chapter and formula index.

## Purpose

State the question the formula answers in one sentence.

## Formula

Write the exact equation in a display math block. Do not round constants here.

## Symbols and units

Define every variable, parameter, type, and unit. Distinguish numerical coordinates
in a chosen unit from dimensional quantities.

## Assumptions and domain

State allowed inputs, nonzero denominators, convergence or smoothness conditions,
coordinate conventions, branch choices, and degeneracies.

## Derivation or proof

Link to the canonical derivation. If a new argument is needed, write it once in
the canonical topic and link from here.

## Worked example and check

Substitute values step by step, preserve exact values, and verify independently.

## Limitations and common errors

Explain when the formula fails or must be modified. Include a counterexample
where that makes the limitation easier to remember.

## Related formulas

Link to existing material. Explain the relationship rather than listing names only.

## References and review

Record a verified source, mathematical review status, and the last real review
date. Do not invent a date or imply computational checks certify every claim.
