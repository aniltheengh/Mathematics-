# Coordinate geometry practice

[Chapter](../../subjects/geometry/coordinate-geometry/cartesian-plane.md) ·
[Practice index](../../indexes/practice.md) · [Solutions](../../solutions/geometry/coordinate-geometry.md)

Assume real coordinates in an orthonormal Euclidean plane, with equal length
units on both axes. All unlabelled quantities use coordinate units. Show the
method, retain exact values, and check results. These are original exercises.

## CG-01 Distance and midpoint

For $A=(-2,1)$ and $B=(4,9)$, find distance and midpoint. Verify that the two half
segments have equal length. [Solution](../../solutions/geometry/coordinate-geometry.md#cg-01-distance-and-midpoint)
