# Mathematics subject map and proposed table of contents

[Home](README.md) · [Available content](INDEX.md)

This is a broad, extensible coverage plan from elementary to advanced university
mathematics, not a claim to list every research specialty. **Available** means
written material exists and is linked. All other entries are **planned**.
The numbered groups are stable navigation identifiers, not prerequisites or
course numbers. No empty topic files are created for the plan.

The organization is an educational design informed by the
[AMS MSC2020 classification](https://mathscinet.ams.org/mathscinet/msc/msc2020.html),
not an official reproduction of its research taxonomy. Subjects overlap; a
canonical explanation has one home and other subjects link to it.

## 01 Foundations, logic, and mathematical language

- Statements, predicates, quantifiers, implication, equivalence, and truth tables
- Sets, membership, subsets, power sets, operations, Cartesian products
- Relations, equivalence classes, order relations, functions, inverse images
- Proof: direct argument, contrapositive, contradiction, induction, counterexamples
- Finite and infinite sets, countability, cardinality, and the axiom of choice
- Formal systems, axioms, models, computability, incompleteness (advanced)
- Category theory: objects, morphisms, functors, natural transformations (advanced)

## 02 Arithmetic and number systems

- Place value, arithmetic operations, order of operations, estimation
- Integers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, proportions
- Divisibility, factors, primes, greatest common divisors, least common multiples
- Signed numbers, absolute value, inequalities, intervals
- Exponents, radicals, scientific notation, significant figures
- Natural, integer, rational, real, and complex number systems
- Units, dimensions, scale, error, and numerical representation

## 03 Elementary and intermediate algebra

- Expressions, identities, factoring, polynomial arithmetic, rational expressions
- Linear equations and inequalities; simultaneous equations
- Quadratic equations, discriminants, completing the square
- Polynomial and rational equations; extraneous roots
- Exponential and logarithmic equations
- Sequences, finite sums, arithmetic and geometric progressions
- Binomial theorem; algebraic inequalities and inequalities with absolute values

## 04 Geometry

- Euclidean foundations: incidence, congruence, similarity, constructions
- Triangles, polygons, circles, area, volume, and solid geometry
- [Available: geometry overview](subjects/geometry/overview.md)
- [Available: Cartesian plane, distance, midpoint, lines, and circles](subjects/geometry/coordinate-geometry/cartesian-plane.md)
- Analytic geometry: loci, intersections, tangency, conics, quadrics
- Polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates; coordinate conversion
- Affine and projective geometry; homogeneous coordinates
- Non-Euclidean geometry and geometric transformations

## 05 Trigonometry

- Angles, degrees, radians, arc length, sectors
- Right-triangle ratios; unit circle and trigonometric functions
- Identities, addition formulas, multiple angles, inverse functions
- Trigonometric equations and inequalities
- Sine and cosine laws; oblique triangles
- Harmonic motion, amplitude, phase, and phasors (links to complex numbers)

## 06 Functions and mathematical analysis foundations

- Domain, codomain, range, graphs, composition, inverses
- Elementary functions and transformations of graphs
- Bounds, monotonicity, periodicity, symmetry, asymptotic notation
- Sequences and convergence; limits of functions; continuity
- Intermediate and extreme value theorems
- Infinite series and convergence tests; power series
- Uniform convergence and exchange of limiting operations (advanced)

## 07 Linear algebra and multilinear algebra

- Vectors, linear combinations, span, independence, bases, dimension
- Matrices, elimination, rank, determinants, linear systems
- Linear maps, change of basis, null spaces and ranges
- Inner products, orthogonality, projections, least squares
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization, spectral theorem
- Singular value decomposition, quadratic forms, positive definiteness
- Dual spaces, bilinear forms, multilinear maps, tensor products
- Exterior algebra and orientation (bridge to geometry)

## 08 Differential and integral calculus

- Derivative as a limit; differentiation rules; implicit differentiation
- Mean value theorem; Taylor theorem and remainder
- Extrema, curve sketching, related rates, sensitivity
- Antiderivatives, definite integrals, Riemann sums, fundamental theorem
- Substitution, integration by parts, rational and trigonometric integration
- Improper integrals; areas, volumes, arc length, work
- Parametric and polar differentiation and integration

## 09 Multivariable and vector calculus

- Functions of several variables, surfaces, limits, continuity
- Partial and directional derivatives, gradients, Jacobians, Hessians
- Total derivative, chain rule, implicit and inverse function theorems
- Multiple integrals, coordinate changes, Jacobian determinants
- Vector fields, line and surface integrals, conservative fields
- Divergence, curl, Green, Gauss, and Stokes theorems
- Differential forms as a unifying language (advanced)

## 10 Ordinary differential equations and dynamical systems

- Modeling, initial and boundary conditions; existence and uniqueness
- First-order separable, linear, exact, and autonomous equations
- Linear higher-order equations, forcing, resonance
- Systems, matrix exponentials, phase portraits
- Laplace transforms; series solutions; special functions
- Stability, linearization, bifurcations, periodic orbits, chaos
- Boundary value problems and Sturm-Liouville theory

## 11 Partial differential equations

- Classification, well-posedness, initial and boundary data
- Transport, wave, heat, and Laplace equations
- Separation of variables; eigenfunction expansions
- Characteristics, Green functions, fundamental solutions
- Weak solutions, distributions, variational formulations
- Elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic theory (advanced)
- Nonlinear conservation laws, shocks, diffusion, reaction systems

## 12 Discrete mathematics and combinatorics

- Counting, permutations, combinations, inclusion-exclusion
- Pigeonhole principle, recurrences, generating functions
- Graphs, trees, paths, connectivity, graph coloring
- Matchings, flows, networks, extremal combinatorics
- Discrete structures, Boolean algebra, finite state models
- Algorithm correctness, asymptotic complexity, discrete optimization
- Design theory and combinatorial probability

## 13 Number theory

- Euclidean algorithm, Bezout identity, congruences, modular arithmetic
- Diophantine equations, Chinese remainder theorem
- Fermat and Euler theorems, arithmetic functions
- Quadratic residues and reciprocity
- Continued fractions, rational approximation
- Algebraic and analytic number theory (advanced)
- Cryptographic mathematics (links to algebra and computation)

## 14 Abstract algebra

- Groups, subgroups, cyclic and permutation groups
- Homomorphisms, quotient groups, group actions
- Rings, ideals, quotient rings, polynomial rings
- Fields, field extensions, finite fields, Galois theory
- Modules and representations (advanced)
- Commutative and homological algebra (advanced)
- Algebraic geometry: polynomial varieties and coordinate rings (advanced)

## 15 Real analysis and measure theory

- Completeness of the real numbers; suprema and infima
- Rigorous sequences, series, limits, and continuity
- Metric spaces, compactness, connectedness, completeness
- Differentiation, integration, function sequences
- Sigma-algebras, measures, Lebesgue integration
- Convergence theorems, product measures, Fubini and Tonelli
- Functions of bounded variation and distributions (advanced)

## 16 Complex analysis

- Complex arithmetic, geometry, polar form, roots
- Analytic functions, Cauchy-Riemann equations
- Contour integration, Cauchy theorem and integral formula
- Taylor and Laurent series, isolated singularities, residues
- Conformal maps and harmonic functions
- Analytic continuation, branches, Riemann surfaces (advanced)

## 17 Probability and stochastic processes

- Sample spaces, events, axioms, conditional probability, independence
- Random variables, distributions, density and mass functions
- Expectation, variance, covariance, conditional expectation
- Transformations and joint distributions
- Laws of large numbers, central limit theorem
- Markov chains, Poisson processes, martingales, Brownian motion
- Stochastic differential equations and stochastic calculus (advanced)

## 18 Statistics and data analysis

- Descriptive statistics, visualization, sampling, study design
- Estimation, likelihood, bias, consistency, confidence intervals
- Hypothesis tests, effect sizes, multiplicity, power
- Linear regression, generalized linear models, ANOVA
- Bayesian inference and computational inference
- Time series, multivariate statistics, statistical learning
- Experimental design, uncertainty, reliability and survival analysis

## 19 Numerical analysis and scientific computation

- Floating-point arithmetic, conditioning, stability, error analysis
- Root finding, interpolation, approximation, numerical differentiation
- Quadrature and adaptive integration
- Direct and iterative linear solvers; numerical eigenproblems
- Numerical ODE and PDE methods, convergence and stability
- Finite differences, finite elements, finite volumes, spectral methods
- Monte Carlo, numerical probability, reproducible computation

## 20 Optimization, operations research, and control mathematics

- Convex sets and functions; unconstrained optimization
- Constrained extrema, Lagrange multipliers, KKT conditions, duality
- Linear, quadratic, integer, nonlinear, and stochastic programming
- Gradient, Newton, and quasi-Newton methods
- Calculus of variations and optimal control
- Dynamic programming, scheduling, decision and game theory
- Controllability, observability, stability, estimation (cross-links to systems)

## 21 Topology

- Topological spaces, bases, continuity, homeomorphisms
- Subspaces, products, quotients, separation axioms
- Compactness, connectedness, covering spaces
- Fundamental group and homotopy
- Homology and cohomology (advanced)
- Manifolds and algebraic topology (advanced)

## 22 Differential geometry and tensor mathematics

- Curves, curvature, torsion; surfaces and fundamental forms
- Smooth manifolds, charts, tangent and cotangent spaces
- Coordinate components versus geometric objects; index notation
- Covariant and contravariant tensors, metrics, contractions
- Connections, covariant derivatives, geodesics, curvature
- Differential forms and integration on manifolds
- Riemannian, symplectic, and Lie group geometry (advanced)

## 23 Functional analysis and operator theory

- Normed and Banach spaces; bounded linear operators
- Hilbert spaces, projections, orthonormal bases
- Duality, Hahn-Banach theorem, weak convergence
- Uniform boundedness, open mapping, closed graph theorems
- Compact and self-adjoint operators; spectral theory
- Sobolev spaces, weak derivatives, variational PDE methods

## 24 Harmonic analysis, transforms, and special functions

- Fourier series and Fourier transforms; convergence and distributions
- Laplace and Z transforms; convolution and transfer functions
- Orthogonal polynomials; Bessel, Legendre, gamma, and beta functions
- Wavelets, time-frequency analysis, sampling theory
- Asymptotic analysis and perturbation methods

## 25 Applied mathematics and mathematical modeling

- Conservation laws, nondimensionalization, scaling, similarity
- Continuum mechanics, fluid and solid mathematical models
- Signals, systems, vibrations, waves, inverse problems
- Calculus of variations in mechanics and field models
- Information theory, coding, mathematical computation
- Mathematical biology, finance, and network models (optional extensions)
- Verification, validation, identifiability, and uncertainty quantification

| Limits and continuity | Analysis foundations; deeper results in real analysis | Calculus, probability, topology |

| Fourier transforms | Harmonic analysis | PDEs, signals, probability |

| Finite element methods | Numerical analysis | PDEs and applied mechanics |

need and reliable sources justify them.
