# Coverage and mathematical proof scope

[Home](README.md) · [Navigation](INDEX.md) · [Subject classification](CONTENTS.md)

This edition supplies 28 written chapters across the 25 branches in the subject map,
including a dedicated conic-sections chapter. Its purpose is a compact lifelong
reference: definitions, conditions, central results, explanations, worked examples,
proofs or derivations, exercises and answers. No numbered subject branch is an empty placeholder.

## What completion means for this edition

Each branch has substantive reference content, with advanced subjects introduced through
their actual mathematical objects and equations. The book does not claim exhaustive
coverage of every research specialty, or full proofs of all deep theorems. A theorem
stated with its hypotheses and cited without proof is distinguished from a proof
developed here. Numerical checks verify encoded cases; they do not certify every claim.

The chapter catalog below is a navigation and size audit, not a mathematical correctness certificate.

## Equations and graphs

Symbolic fractions, radicals, constants and identities are retained where available.
A plotted curve is sampled using floating-point arithmetic from its stated equation;
no finite digital picture is itself an exact continuous curve. Geometric plane plots
use equal length scales. Graphs identify parameters, axes, discontinuities and units where relevant.
Reproducible plotting scripts and the [figure gallery](indexes/figures.md) connect each image to its source chapter.

## Continued maintenance

Errors should be corrected with a focused commit and an explicit check. Specialist
proof development can expand these chapters without changing their canonical subject homes.
Personal mastery is recorded separately and is not inferred from the presence of written material.
