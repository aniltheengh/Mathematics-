# Lightweight revision method

[Home](../README.md) · [Progress tracker](progress.csv) · [Learning route](learning-path.md)

Use the encyclopedia as a reference and a source of short practice sessions.
You do not have to restart an entire textbook when only one idea is rusty.

## Five personal statuses

| CSV value | Meaning | Evidence needed to move forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `not-started` | Not yet studied or reassessed | Attempt a diagnostic |
| `reviewing` | Currently rebuilding the idea | Read the definition, argument, and one example |
| `with-notes` | Can explain and solve with notes open | Close notes and attempt a fresh problem |
| `independent` | Can explain conditions and solve without help | Recheck on a later date with changed numbers |
| `needs-practice` | A gap or repeated error remains | Name the gap and solve one focused follow-up |

These are self-assessments, not certifications. Returning to `needs-practice`
after forgetting is useful information. A chapter's content status says whether
it is written; it says nothing about the learner's mastery.

## One session

1. Choose one small topic or section and try one problem with notes closed.
2. If stuck, read only the missing definition or proof step, then one worked example.
3. Close the notes and solve a changed-number or conceptual version.
4. Update one CSV row: status, real review date, next review date, evidence, and
   the next action. Use ISO dates such as `2026-10-17` when that is the actual date.

Start with about 15–25 minutes. As a practical scheduling heuristic, revisit a
newly recovered idea after roughly 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, and 3 months. These are
adjustable reminders, not a scientifically optimal schedule. Shorten the gap
after a failure and lengthen it after successful recall.

Dates in the supplied tracker are blank because no study session has been
observed. Only enter dates after actually reviewing. `next_review` can remain
blank until a real plan exists.

## Maintenance

The CSV has one row per topic, not one row per exercise. Keep evidence concrete,
for example “CG-03 solved without notes; confused line and segment in CG-12.”
Quote CSV fields that contain commas. Git history preserves earlier assessments.
Do not record account credentials or sensitive personal data in a public tracker.
