# Geometry

[Home](../../README.md) · [Navigation](../../INDEX.md) · [Subject map](../../CONTENTS.md)

Geometry studies shape, size, position, and the structures that make those ideas
precise. Different geometric settings use different assumptions; results about
a flat Euclidean plane do not automatically apply on a curved surface.

| Viewpoint | Main question | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Euclidean geometry | What follows from lengths, angles, and geometric axioms? | Pythagorean theorem |
| Coordinate geometry | How do numbers represent positions and figures? | Find a midpoint from two coordinate pairs |
| Analytic geometry | What sets of points do equations describe? | Intersect a line and a circle |
| Differential geometry | How do smooth curves, surfaces, and spaces bend? | Curvature of a pipe centerline |
| Topology | What survives continuous deformation? | Whether a surface has a hole |

Coordinate geometry is a method within analytic geometry, not a separate universe
of formulas. Coordinates describe geometric objects; changing coordinates changes
their descriptions but need not change the objects.
